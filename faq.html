Store Policies & Ordering

We accept returns on all factory-sealed board games within 30 days of purchase with a valid receipt. Opened games cannot be returned unless they are missing pieces or have defective components, in which case we will assist you in contacting the publisher for replacements.
Yes, we price match with all local brick-and-mortar competitors within a 25-mile radius. However, we do not price match with online-only retailers like Amazon, as our pricing reflects the value of our community space, expert staff, and in-store events.
You can pre-order upcoming releases in-store or through our online portal. We require a 20% deposit to secure your copy. Once the item arrives, you will receive an email and have 14 days to pick it up and pay the remaining balance.
We currently buy TCG singles (Magic, Pokémon, Lorcana) for store credit or cash based on TCGPlayer market pricing. We periodically host "Flea Market" days where you can sell your used board games directly to other community members, but the store itself does not buy used board games.
Absolutely! If our distributors have it, we can get it for you. Special orders typically take 1-2 weeks to arrive. Stop by the front desk or use the contact form to make a request.
We offer flat-rate standard shipping within the continental US, and free shipping on orders over $150. Expedited shipping is available at checkout. Local customers can also choose free In-Store Pickup.
Category 2

Events & In-Store Play

Open play tables are free to use during normal business hours on a first-come, first-served basis, unless there is a scheduled ticketed event. We also offer private VIP rooms that can be reserved for $20/hour.
No! We have a massive library of over 500 open board games that you can play for free while in the store. Of course, you are always welcome to bring your own games from home.
We allow outside snacks and covered drinks, provided you clean up after yourself. We also sell a selection of snacks, sodas, and energy drinks at the front counter. Greasy foods are strictly prohibited near the game library.
Absolutely. Our community is incredibly welcoming to new players. We even host specific "Learn to Play" and casual format events alongside our more competitive tournaments. Just let the judge know you're new!
The best way is to join our official Discord server, which has a dedicated LFG (Looking for Group) channel. We also host Adventurers League on Wednesday nights, which is a drop-in format perfect for meeting other players.
Yes, we are a family-friendly environment. However, children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. We host a specific "Family Game Day" on the first Sunday of every month.
