Who We Are

About Our Organization

BoardCraze is an independent, community-driven tabletop gaming retailer dedicated to bringing analog entertainment to the modern era. We curate the largest selection of strategy board games, card games, and hobby supplies in the region.

Unlike big-box retailers, our organization is run entirely by active gamers. Every product on our shelves has been vetted, played, and loved by our staff. We pride ourselves on creating an inclusive environment where veterans and newcomers can sit at the same table and share an adventure.

Our Mission

To provide the highest quality tabletop games and hobby accessories while fostering a welcoming, educational, and thrilling environment. We aim to pull people away from their screens and bring them face-to-face across a table.

Our Vision

To become the premier destination for analog gaming in the country. We envision a community where every neighborhood has a BoardCraze tavern—a place where imagination, strategy, and camaraderie intersect on a nightly basis.

Our History

BoardCraze opened its doors in a tiny 500 sq-ft garage, stocking only MTG cards and a few classic Catan boxes.

2019 - Expanding the Tavern

Moved to our current flagship location, adding dedicated play tables and hosting our first regional tournament.
